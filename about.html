The hotel

Accommodation is the beginning, not the whole visit.

Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites is a hospitality and lifestyle destination in Onitsha. Guests stay, dine, relax, celebrate, connect and belong under one roof.

Every guest deserves royal treatment and exceptional value. The rooms run from Portsmouth Royal to the Senatorial Suite.

A stay includes breakfast for one, high-speed internet, and access to the hotel’s leisure and entertainment.
