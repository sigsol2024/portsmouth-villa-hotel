Privacy Policy

How an enquiry is kept.

Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites handles personal information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act. This page is the hotel’s current notice, not a lawyer’s opinion.

Who we are

What an enquiry collects

The enquiry form asks for your name, phone number, dates, and whether you are asking about a room or Chidera Hall. You may add a note. The hotel uses those details to reply. It does not run a mailing list from this website.

The booking bar

The date bar on the homepage and the room page is provided by StayEazi for the time being. Dates you enter there are handled by that service as well as by this hotel once a booking is made. When the hotel’s own booking tool replaces it, this notice will be updated.

Sharing, keeping and security

Enquiry details are shared with the staff who answer them. They are kept for as long as the enquiry or the stay requires, and then removed. The hotel takes reasonable care to keep them from being lost or misused.

Your rights

You may ask what information the hotel holds about you, ask for a correction, or ask for it to be deleted where the law allows. Write to .

Cookies

This website uses only what it needs to load. It does not run its own analytics.

Children

The enquiry form is for adults making a booking. The hotel does not knowingly collect a child’s details through this site.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will replace this page.
