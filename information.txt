PORTSMOUTH VILLA HOTEL & SUITES REDEFINES HOSPITALITY IN ONITSHA AS NOLLYWOOD TEEN STAR KAMDEBBIE EMERGES BRAND AMBASSADOR

IN THE CITY OF ONITSHA, ONE DESTINATION REIGNS SUPREME — PORTSMOUTH VILLA HOTEL & SUITES

Onitsha, Anambra State — A new chapter is unfolding in the hospitality landscape of Onitsha, and at the heart of that transformation is Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites — a premium hospitality and lifestyle destination redefining the meaning of comfort, entertainment, leisure and memorable guest experiences.

In a city renowned for commerce, enterprise and vibrant social life, Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites is positioning itself as more than a place to sleep. It is a destination where guests can stay, dine, relax, celebrate, connect, entertain and belong — all under one roof.

Its bold proposition says it all:

“IN THE CITY OF ONITSHA, ONE DESTINATION REIGNS SUPREME.”

And Portsmouth is determined to make that statement an experience guests can see, feel and remember.

A NEW STANDARD OF HOSPITALITY

According to management, Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites was conceived around a simple but ambitious vision: to move beyond the traditional hotel model and create a complete hospitality destination where accommodation is only the beginning.

From elegant accommodation and refined dining to rooftop entertainment, poolside relaxation, nightlife, private experiences and events, Portsmouth brings multiple dimensions of hospitality together in one carefully designed environment.

The result is a destination that combines comfort, style, entertainment, convenience and value while maintaining a warm and distinctly Nigerian hospitality character.

LUXURY MEETS VALUE

One of Portsmouth's strongest propositions is its ability to combine an upscale experience with exceptional value.

Whether visiting Onitsha for business, shopping, leisure, celebration, family occasions or simply a well-deserved escape from the pace of the city, guests have access to thoughtfully positioned accommodation categories designed around different levels of comfort, purpose and preference.

PORTSMOUTH ROYAL

Your Royal Comfort Starts Here.

The Portsmouth Royal is designed for guests who value comfort, privacy, neatness and excellent service. It is an ideal choice for business travellers, couples, leisure guests and short-stay visitors seeking quality without unnecessary extravagance.

DIPLOMATIC ROOM

Distinguished Comfort for Distinguished Guests.

The Diplomatic Room delivers a more elevated experience for executives, corporate travellers, professionals and distinguished visitors who appreciate sophistication, privacy and refined hospitality.

AMBASSADORIAL SUITE

Where Every Stay Becomes a Statement.

Designed for senior executives, VIP guests, special occasions and memorable stays, the Ambassadorial Suite offers an enhanced level of comfort and distinction.

SENATORIAL SUITE

The Executive Experience.

At the pinnacle of Portsmouth's accommodation offering is the Senatorial Suite — an elevated experience created for VIPs, high-profile travellers, executives and guests celebrating significant milestones.

Across the accommodation experience, guests can enjoy complimentary breakfast for one, high-speed internet, access to recreational facilities, professional hospitality services and access to the hotel's exciting leisure and entertainment offerings.

ONE DESTINATION. NINE SIGNATURE EXPERIENCES.

What truly distinguishes Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites is its Nine Signature Experiences, bringing accommodation, dining, relaxation, entertainment and events together as one integrated lifestyle destination.

1. THE RECEPTION

The experience begins from the moment guests arrive — with a warm, welcoming and professional front-of-house environment designed to create a positive first impression.

2. THE ACCOMMODATION

Thoughtfully designed rooms and suites offering comfort, privacy and a range of options to suit different guest needs.

3. PORTSMOUTH LOUNGE

A comfortable social space for relaxation, conversation, meetings and unwinding in an elegant atmosphere.

4. THE VILLA TABLE

A dining experience designed to bring together great food, quality service and an inviting atmosphere for individuals, families, couples and corporate guests.

5. SKYDECK POOL & BAR

An elevated leisure experience where guests can relax by the pool, enjoy refreshments and soak in the atmosphere away from the city's hustle and bustle.

6. ROOFTOP VIBE

Food, music, drinks, social connection and entertainment come together in a rooftop experience designed for guests looking to unwind and enjoy the city's vibrant energy.

7.   FESTCAVE CLUB ( VILLA AFTER DARK) 

When the evening comes alive, Portsmouth transforms into an exciting nightlife destination, offering music, entertainment and an energetic atmosphere for night-time revelry.

8. THE PORTSMOUTH PRIVÉ

A more exclusive experience for guests seeking privacy, special celebrations, intimate gatherings and premium moments away from the crowd.

9.    CHIDERA HALL ( THE VILLA GRAND HALL) 

A stylish event venue designed for weddings, birthdays, conferences, corporate events, meetings and celebrations, with capacity for approximately 150 guests.

Together, these nine experiences create what Portsmouth describes as a complete hospitality and lifestyle ecosystem — one destination with something for almost every occasion.

KAMDEBBIE JOINS THE PORTSMOUTH FAMILY

Adding another exciting dimension to the brand's growing profile, Nollywood teenage sensation Deborah Okolie, popularly known as KamDebbie, has emerged as an official Brand Ambassador of Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites.

The Imo State-born young entertainer has continued to attract attention with her growing presence in Nigeria's entertainment industry.

Having progressed from child acting into a broader creative career encompassing acting, music and brand influencing, KamDebbie represents a new generation of Nigerian entertainment talent.

Her screen presence, youthful energy and versatility have helped establish her as one of the promising young personalities attracting attention across the Nigerian entertainment space.

Her partnership with Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites represents a strategic alignment between youth, entertainment, lifestyle, excellence and hospitality.

With KamDebbie becoming one of the faces associated with the Portsmouth brand, the hotel is strengthening its connection with a younger, vibrant and increasingly influential audience.

LEADERSHIP WITH VISION

Behind the Portsmouth experience is a leadership team committed to building a distinctive hospitality brand.

The hotel is owned by Chief Amb. Festus Okolie Udungwo, who serves as Chief Executive Officer, while Rev. Amb. Zeal Festus Okolie serves as Managing Director.

Under their hands-on leadership and commitment to service excellence, Portsmouth Villa Hotel & Suites is steadily establishing itself as a destination for accommodation, dining, entertainment, leisure and events in Onitsha.

Their philosophy is straightforward:

“Every guest deserves royal treatment and exceptional value.”
